Class projects

Design work from mechanical engineering courses at Michigan State.

A

Inline three-cylinder engine block drawing

A hand-drafted multiview drawing of an inline three-cylinder engine block. Using third-angle orthographic projection, I developed top and front views that define the cylinder bores, crankshaft and main bearing locations, and the ports on top of the block. An isometric sketch shows the overall form, and center and dimension lines lay out the geometry for a CAD model.

  • Hand drafting
  • Orthographic projection
  • Isometric sketching
B

Gearbox assembly and drawing

A gearbox assembly modeled in Siemens NX. I built the housing, base, end covers and inspection cover, along with the internal gears, shafts and bearings, as separate parts and joined them with assembly constraints. From the model, I produced a matching drawing with top, front and side views, an isometric view and key dimensions.

  • Siemens NX
  • Assembly constraints
  • Drafting
